ESCALATION AND STOP SAFETY POLICY

1. Scope

This policy defines the universal safety guardrails governing automated
revenue recovery decisions across all payment instruments.

This policy takes precedence over instrument-specific recovery policies.

2. Mandatory Escalation Triggers

A transaction MUST be escalated for manual review when:

- fraud_probability > 0.70
- transaction amount > INR 50,000

Automated recovery must not be executed before the required review.

3. Mandatory Stop Triggers

Automated recovery MUST STOP when:

- attempt_number >= 2
- recovery_probability < 0.30
- transaction status is already SUCCESS
- transaction status cannot be safely determined
- the transaction has already been successfully debited
- the failure is classified as non-retryable

4. Priority Order

Safety rules must be evaluated before any recovery action.

Priority:

1. SUCCESS / completed transaction → STOP
2. Fraud risk > 0.70 → ESCALATE
3. Amount > INR 50,000 → ESCALATE
4. Retry limit reached → STOP
5. Recovery probability < 0.30 → STOP
6. Otherwise → evaluate instrument-specific policy

5. Action Recommendation

fraud_probability > 0.70
    → ESCALATE

amount > 50,000
    → ESCALATE

attempt_number >= 2
    → STOP

recovery_probability < 0.30
    → STOP

Otherwise:
    → Continue to instrument-specific recovery policy.

6. Safety Principle

AI recommendations must never override these safety rules.

The deterministic safety policy always takes precedence over the AI agent.
