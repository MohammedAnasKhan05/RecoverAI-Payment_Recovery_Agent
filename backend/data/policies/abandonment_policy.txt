CHECKOUT ABANDONMENT POLICY

1. Scope

This policy governs recovery actions for customers who start checkout
but do not complete the payment.

2. Permitted Recovery Actions

RecoverAI may send payment reminders through:

- WhatsApp
- SMS
- Email

3. Reminder Schedule

First reminder:

30 minutes after checkout abandonment.

Second reminder:

24 hours after abandonment if payment remains incomplete.

Maximum reminders:

2.

4. Stopping Conditions

The reminder sequence MUST stop when:

- payment is completed
- customer explicitly cancels
- recovery_probability < 0.30
- maximum reminder count is reached
- fraud risk > 0.70

5. Fraud Handling

High-risk abandoned transactions must not receive automated recovery
reminders.

Action:

→ ESCALATE

6. Action Recommendation

failure_reason = abandoned
    → SEND_REMINDER

failure_reason = pending
    → SEND_REMINDER / VERIFY_STATUS

failure_reason = drop_off
    → SEND_REMINDER

reminder_count >= 2
    → STOP

recovery_probability < 0.30
    → STOP

fraud_probability > 0.70
    → ESCALATE

7. Customer Experience

Messages should be concise and should provide the customer with a
secure way to return to the existing checkout session.

Do not create unnecessary duplicate payment sessions.
