CARD FAILURE ALTERNATE PAYMENT POLICY

1. Scope

This policy governs failed or declined credit card and debit card
transactions.

2. Retryable Conditions

A card transaction may be considered for one controlled retry when:

- the failure is classified as a temporary technical failure, and
- the transaction has not already succeeded, and
- all safety guardrails pass.

Maximum automatic retry attempts for the MVP: 1.

3. Non-Retryable Conditions

The following failures should not be repeatedly retried:

- Insufficient funds
- Do not honor
- 3DS authentication failure
- Card blocked or expired
- Invalid card details
- Customer explicitly declines the payment

The system should offer an alternate payment method instead.

4. Alternate Payment Methods

Possible alternatives include:

- UPI
- Netbanking
- Another saved debit/credit card
- UPI QR / intent

5. Customer Experience

Do not repeatedly attempt the same declined card.

Where appropriate, present the customer with an alternate payment option.

6. Action Recommendation

failure_reason = card_declined
    → ALTERNATE_PAYMENT

temporary technical failure
    → RETRY

retry limit reached
    → STOP

high fraud risk
    → ESCALATE

high-value transaction
    → ESCALATE
